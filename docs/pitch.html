Problem

"Proof of participation" NFTs are given to anyone who clicks — so they prove nothing. Ecosystems need a credential that carries information.

Product

A non-transferable ERC-721 earned by passing a 5-question BOT Chain quiz. One per wallet, soulbound forever — the badge is evidence of knowledge, not attendance.

How it works

A backend scores the quiz server-side (the answer key never reaches the browser) and issues an EIP-191 voucher bound to your wallet. The contract verifies the signature at mint.

Why BOT Chain

Every mint is a real mainnet transaction. The quiz teaches the chain — gas token, chain ID, explorer, leaderboard — turning a badge claim into onboarding.
